## Who to talk to

Our welfare officer is **Lizzie Cox**. If you have any concerns about the
welfare of yourself or anyone else at the club, contact Lizzie directly.

The welfare officer is the lead for the club on any safeguarding matter,
covering both adults and under-18s.

## What happens next

Any concern, allegation or report relating to the welfare of children or
vulnerable adults is recorded and responded to promptly, following the club's
safeguarding policy. Complaints about the behaviour of members or officers are
handled through the club's disciplinary process, which is set out in full in the
[Rules and Constitution](/rules-and-constitution).

## If it's urgent

If someone is in immediate danger, contact the emergency services on 999.

For concerns about a child where there is no immediate danger, Somerset
Council's children's social care team can be contacted directly, and you do not
have to go through the club first.
