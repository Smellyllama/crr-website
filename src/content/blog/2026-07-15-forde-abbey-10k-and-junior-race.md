In a change of pace, a report from one of our own races!

Forde Abbey always receives fantastic feedback from the local running community, attracting seasoned club runners for the 10k, newer runners looking for something more challenging, and the local families for the junior race who make the evening such a special event on the calendar.

That being the case, putting on a great event every year comes with a lot of pressure, and mostly that falls on our Race Director Simon Hall. This year we had to contend with a red weather warning, which meant we had to postpone the event to ensure it could be run safely. Then sod's law, the only day we could move it to there was some kind of major sporting event, which was almost as important as the Forde Abbey races themselves!

Thank you to everyone who engaged with us in such a supportive way whilst we worked through the significant logistics of moving the date, rearranging all the timing, catering, venue, marshals, and of course the many, many race entry transfers and refunds.

Forde Abbey 10k is not a profit making exercise. We make very little profit from the event and the vast majority of that covers the timing and bespoke medals for the free junior race, which I'm sure you'll agree makes your 10k entry fee an absolute bargain!

Full results from the night can be found on the Timing Monkey website — thank you Matt for the fantastically smooth operation — and loads of photos can be found on our Facebook group and page, but here are the highlights.

## Junior Race

For safeguarding reasons I won't post details of the children here, but a fantastic turnout again this year with exactly 100 finishers and brilliant representation from all the local schools.
