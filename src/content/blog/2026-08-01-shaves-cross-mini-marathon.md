Shaves Cross Mini Marathon, which unlike the name would suggest is definitely not a marathon.

Five miles starting with a lap and a half of a very rough field before heading out onto the road for the majority of the route — most of which felt like it was uphill! A great fast descent before a sneaky final hill and heading back into the field. If your ankles survived the first lap and a half there was still the final half lap to go to get to the finishing straight, which following the theme of the rest of the race was also uphill!

Despite the hills, a great race was had by all, but especially Steve who had to get his elbows out in a sprint finish to remember!

No times for this old school race, just numbers on a whiteboard — so finishing order.

A great Saturday evening supporting a historic and local event, with a BBQ and beer tent afterwards. Look forward to next year!
