CRR's were represented at only one race this weekend with Adrian Male racing the New Dark Valley Half Marathon held at the Moors Valley Country Park Ringwood. This was a challenging hilly offroad trail race in the dark from start to finish, setting off with 300+ other half Marathon runners Adrian set off in a blaze of head torches into the forest trails and mud, with the heavy rain of the previous 24hrs the going was soft and Adrian made excellent progress up through the field, at the 7 1/2 mile marker Adrian was in the top 50 runners and feeling strong as the hills were taking their toll on some of the front runners, with the going becoming a little firmer coming towards the end Adrian was really making up ground and with a sprint finish crossed the line in 1h 50m 37s and 39/312
