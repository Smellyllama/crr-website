A fairly quiet weekend with the weather changing. It was down to Nina Wagstaff to represent CRR in the first event, heading off to the Avon Valley Runners Wiltshire Half Marathon based out of Edington near Trowbridge.

Nina, having been suffering with a virus, took the gamble to run this popular event. Heading out from the start she took a more cautious approach, finding her pace over the first few miles. With the hills claiming some of the early sprinters, Nina was starting to feel her racing pace returning and took to push hard and move up the field with confidence. With the miles passing she had made up some good places, and heading into the finish Nina crossed the line in 1:31:50, 53rd of 336 and 2nd in category.

The Bicton Blister 10 mile off road race was the second event for CRR, heading off to Bicton Gardens, East Budleigh, was Wayne Loveridge.

This race starts in the grounds of the gardens and then heads out for a short road section. Wayne was off hard from the start, and with the road section cleared he took to the trails and tracks around Woodbury Common. With the gradient changes the going had definitely started to take its toll, but Wayne was in his element and was chasing down the very competitive field of front runners to move into the top five. With the route peaking at eight miles, the smoother and more predictable paths back down towards the finish line allowed Wayne's smooth running style to come to the fore, and with a final sprint over the last road section he crossed in 1:03:26, 3rd of 439.
