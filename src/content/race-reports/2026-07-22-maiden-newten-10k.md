Last one. Maiden NewTen. Claire Gibbs was the only one to do the race last year, as it clashed with a pub run, but she was really enthusiastic about the route — so as it didn't clash this time, I signed up. Then the weather happened, things got moved, and this year two of us made it to the start line. Just.

The runners were being led to the start as we arrived, so I grabbed our numbers and we set off trying to find them.

The first half mile is down. The next mile and a half is up, with no overtaking for most of it. Then it's downhill until the very end. The steep sided valley we ran through reminded me of the Giant's Head course, and some of the route covered bits of the Hillfort Ultra route.

It was a thoroughly enjoyable race, which I hope to do again next year.
